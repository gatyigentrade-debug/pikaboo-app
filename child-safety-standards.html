Child Safety Standards Policy

App Name: PikaBoo

Developer: Nkosinathi Magatya / GatyiTrade

At PikaBoo, we maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are entirely committed to keeping our platform safe and secure for all eligible users, and we explicitly prohibit any form of content, behavior, or interaction that exploits, abuses, or endangers minors.

Prohibited Conduct

  • Any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Grooming, sextortion, or any attempt to initiate inappropriate contact with minors.
  • Exploiting or endangering minors in any manner.

Reporting & Enforcement

If you encounter or suspect any violations of these standards, you can immediately report them via our in-app reporting tools or directly by contacting our support team at our developer email address. Any accounts or content found violating these standards will be permanently banned and reported to relevant authorities (such as law enforcement or NCMEC).

Point of Contact

For any child safety concerns or official inquiries, you can reach out via the developer contact email associated with this Google Play account.
